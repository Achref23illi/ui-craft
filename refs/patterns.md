# Pattern catalogue — where to look

Each pattern names the apps (and sheet numbers) that do it well, best first. Open a sheet with Read:
`refs/sheets/<id>-<App>-<nn>.jpg` (six screens of one app). `scripts/refs.py "<words>"` finds the
lines and sheets for you. Measured recipes by screen type are in `refs/recipes.md`; per-app
numbers in `refs/notes.md`. 78 apps, 4,536 screens, 760 sheets.
